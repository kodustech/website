What to post

You can post only about Kodus or include it in comparisons and tool lists. What you say and how you say it is up to you. After all, you know your audience better than we do.

post_types.md
TypeExampleImpressions counted
Dedicated postA post only about Kodus: your experience, a feature you tried or a real code review100%
ComparisonKodus compared with another code review tool50%
Tool listA list of AI code review tools that includes Kodus50%

Example: in one month you publish three posts with 10k impressions each. The dedicated post counts 10k, the comparison counts 5k and the tool list counts 5k.
In total, that's 20k counted impressions, worth $100.

allowed.txt

The Rules

  • Your audience should primarily be developers, engineering leads, or people who work in software.
  • Tag @kodustech in your posts.
  • To avoid spam, leave at least 2 days between posts about Kodus on the same platform.
  • Include your personalized link so we can track signups.
not_paid.txt

What Doesn't Get Paid

  • Posts from accounts that don't primarily reach developers.
  • Posts published before you joined the program.
  • Paid or boosted impressions. Only organic reach counts.
  • Reposted content you've already submitted for payment.
  • False claims about other tools.
  • Bought engagement or bot-generated comments.
  • Fake or duplicate signups. These don't count toward the leaderboard and may disqualify you from the monthly bonus.
